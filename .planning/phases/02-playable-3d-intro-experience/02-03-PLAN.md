<objective>
The tracer for this phase: one real path from the title screen through the main menu into a
playable enclosed 3D level, with the verification chain retargeted so it stays honest once the
boot scene stops being a 3D scene.

Three things have to move together. `intro_level.tscn` must exist before the project verifier
can point its Node3D assertion at it (D-27), and that retarget must land before
`run/main_scene` becomes the `Control`-rooted title screen, or the whole check goes red. D-27
explicitly rejects the cheap alternative — wrapping the title UI in a Node3D shell — because
that makes the assertion pass without asserting anything, which is the same vacuity failure a
Phase 1 code review already caught once.

This plan also closes the first of the two gaps `02-VALIDATION.md` names. `probe_camiel_movement.gd`
proves movement, jump and camera-follow against `test_space.tscn`, but INTRO-01 and INTRO-02
name the intro level. The call taken here is to **close the gap rather than accept Phase 1
coverage**: the intro level is fully enclosed where the test space has open edges, so its wall
geometry and its spring-arm camera behaviour are genuinely different conditions worth proving.
The probe is parametrized to run a portable subset of its cases against each listed scene; the
test-space-specific cases, which depend on an open edge and a single back wall, stay where
they are.

The main menu in this plan carries its Start control only. The audio panel and its sliders are
the whole subject of plan 02-05 and land in this same phase — this is the thin end of a tracer,
not a reduced menu.

Purpose: after this plan a child can start the game, reach the menu, press Start, and walk and
jump around a room they cannot fall out of. Everything plan 02-04 adds hangs off this skeleton.

Output: `scenes/intro_level.tscn`, `scenes/title_screen.tscn` with `scripts/title_screen.gd`,
`scenes/main_menu.tscn` with `scripts/main_menu.gd`, a retargeted `verify_3d_project.gd`, a
parametrized `probe_camiel_movement.gd`, and a new `scripts/tools/probe_screen_flow.gd`.

Decisions implemented: D-13, D-14, D-15, D-17, D-27, D-28, D-29. Level geometry within D-17's
enclosed shape is Claude's discretion.
