<objective>
Give the volume control a place a child and a co-present adult can actually reach, and record
the verification chain's two remaining soft spots as executable cases.

D-25 puts the sound slider on the main menu because INTRO-06 requires it to have an audible
effect, and a control nobody can reach cannot have one. The music slider next to it is
additive: the autoload already exposes a music volume setter, the second row costs almost
nothing, and it lets an adult quiet the music while keeping the effect feedback audible.

The second half of this plan closes the second gap `02-VALIDATION.md` names. The headless
check's non-vacuity guard only fails when the probe glob matches nothing at all. This phase
takes the project from one probe to four, so deleting any single one now leaves the glob
non-empty and the check green with a whole file's assertions silently gone. The call taken here
is the in-scope minimum the validation strategy specifies: **record the weakness as a passing
self-test case that says plainly what it documents, and do not implement the named-probe
allow-list**, which is a change to the check's own contract and larger than this phase. Making
that case assert a failure without building the allow-list would claim a guard this project
does not have — the same shape of dishonesty as the vacuous empty-glob pass a Phase 1 code
review already caught.

Purpose: this plan runs beside plan 02-04 in the same wave and shares no file with it.

Output: the audio panel inside `scenes/main_menu.tscn` with its wiring in `scripts/main_menu.gd`,
three new cases in `scripts/tools/probe_audio_buses.gd`, and two new cases in
`scripts/tools/test_headless_check.sh`.

Decisions implemented: D-23, D-25, D-26. Slider range, defaults and the presence of a second
row are the Claude's-Discretion area the UI contract resolved.
</objective>
