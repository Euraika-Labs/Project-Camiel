Close the two dormant defects that block every audio assertion in this phase, then rebuild the
`AudioManager` autoload on top of them.

The three committed `.ogg` files are Opus inside an Ogg container while their `.import`
sidecars declare the Vorbis importer with `valid=false`; the engine skips them silently, so
`load()` returns `null` and prints an `ERROR:` line the moment anything touches them.

4.7.2 — the engine reports one bus, `Master`, so `get_bus_index("SFX")` returns `-1`.
Neither failure is visible to any existing gate today.

can be proved headlessly, and both are unreachable until these two fixes land. Every later
plan that plays a sound depends on this one.

`res://default_bus_layout.tres`, a rebuilt `scripts/audio_manager.gd` autoload keeping the
archived public surface, and `scripts/tools/probe_audio_buses.gd` asserting all of it inside
the existing headless check.
