<objective>
Give the room an ending. A finish marker that emits a signal, a win overlay that listens for it,
one collectible with a pickup sound that plays exactly once, and a replay that restarts the
level in place.

Each phrase in these requirements maps to a recorded defect rather than to style. "A single
signal-driven code path rather than a direct cross-script call" (INTRO-04) exists because the
archived finish marker declared a signal it never emitted and instead reached through a group
into the main script's method. "The pickup sound plays exactly once per pickup" (INTRO-03)
exists because two independent scripts both played a sound off the same signal, one of them
through a player whose stream was never set. Engine testing during research ruled out the two
obvious suspects — multiple collision shapes do not double-fire an area's body signal, and a
duplicated connection is refused outright with a loud error — which leaves the real cause
exactly where the concern record put it: two listeners. The fix is structural, and it is
asserted rather than assumed.

Purpose: this closes INTRO-03, INTRO-04 and the win-screen half of INTRO-05, and it completes
the loop a child plays. It runs beside plan 02-05 in the same wave; the two touch no file in
common.

Output: `scenes/finish_marker.tscn` and `scripts/finish_marker.gd`, `scenes/collectible.tscn`
and `scripts/collectible.gd`, `scripts/intro_level.gd`, the win overlay inside
`scenes/intro_level.tscn`, and three new cases in `scripts/tools/probe_screen_flow.gd`.

Decisions implemented: D-14, D-15, D-18, D-19, D-20, D-21, D-28, D-29.
